About Me

I'm a Senior Java Developer with over 8 years of hands-on experience building and scaling enterprise-level applications. My expertise spans Java SE/EE, Spring Boot, Microservices architecture, and a wide array of full-stack and cloud technologies. I specialize in crafting robust, secure, and high-performance systems, with strong command over RESTful and SOAP web services, JPA/Hibernate, and CI/CD pipelines using tools like Jenkins, Git, Maven, Docker, and Kubernetes. My backend strength is complemented by front-end proficiency in HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, jQuery, JSP, and modern JavaScript frameworks. Whether it’s optimizing high-throughput applications, designing scalable microservices, or collaborating within Agile teams, I bring a results-driven mindset and a passion for clean, maintainable code. I thrive in solving complex problems and delivering value through innovation, precision, and strong stakeholder communication.
